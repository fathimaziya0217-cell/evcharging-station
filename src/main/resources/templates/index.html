How EV Charging Booking Works

Reserve guaranteed charging slots in just 4 simple steps without waiting in long queues.

1. Find Station

Locate nearby charging stations in Kerala or other major cities using the interactive map or city filters.

2. Select Charger

Pick a compatible charger (CCS2, Type 2, CHAdeMO) matching your EV's power and connector requirements.

3. Choose Time Slot

Select an available time slot. Our concurrency lock guarantees no double-booking for the selected gun.

4. Pay & Charge

Complete payment via Demo simulation or gateway, receive your instant reference, and drive in to charge.
